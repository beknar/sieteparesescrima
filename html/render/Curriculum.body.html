Curriculum Outline for Siete Pares Promotion

Note: the descriptions are NOT the full sinawali but are reminders for what and how they are supposed to be done. This is not intended as a guide to Sinawali.

  • Calisthenics - stretching and warmup
    • Neck and head exercise
    • Shoulder exercise
    • Wrist exercise
    • Knuckle pushups (concrete and sand)
    • Finger pushups
    • Sit-ups
    • Knee rotation exercise
    • Leg stretching - side lunges / kicks
    • Body Tempering - forearms, chest, stomach, back, makiwara (fists)
  • White Belt Requirement

  • Black Belt Requirement
    • Solo Baston Drill
      • First Eye : 7 strikes - diagonal strikes, sak-sak
      • Second : 7 strikes - abaniko
      • Third Eye : 7 strikes - payong
      • Fourth Pattern: 7 strikes - pitic, witic, orabis
      • Fifth Pattern: 7 strikes - figure 8 and avedesario: little a, big a
      • Sixth Pattern: 7 strikes - lightning: diagonal, reverse sak-sak, florete, lightning
      • Seventh Pattern: 12 strikes carenza: diagonal, reverse sak-sak, florete, 3x abaniko, redonda, figure 8
    • Sinawali Drills
      • Pahipo : 7 strikes
      • Ginunting : 7 strikes
      • Lightning : 7 strikes

      • Estokada saksak sungket

      • Two Daggers

      • Mid-range sak-sak defense
      • Bottom sak-sak defense
      • Small circle defense
    • Slash and Cover Knife Drill
    • Tres Kantos Knife Drill

    • Special Sinawali Drills
      • Ikog pagi strike (stingray strike)

      • Heaven Six broken
      • Reverse Heaven Six
      • Buhawi two sticks sinawali drill - 12 strikes

      • Sagang Labo - machete vs empty hand

      • Sagang - elbow vs empty hand
